Diagnostic Services

Advanced imaging solutions providing crystalline clarity for precision diagnostics. Our state-of-the-art modalities are designed for both rigorous clinical accuracy and optimal patient comfort. Explore our comprehensive suite of diagnostic services below.

View Modalities
HIGH FIELD

MRI - High Field Imaging

High resolution, multi-planar imaging utilizing powerful magnetic fields. Ideal for detailed neurological, musculoskeletal, or soft tissue evaluations without ionizing radiation.

Preparation

Remove all metallic objects. Fasting may be required depending on the use of contrast agents.

FAST-SCAN

CT Scans - Fast Acquisition

Rapid, cross-sectional X-ray imaging providing detailed 3D views of internal structures, crucial for urgent diagnostics.

Preparation

Prep: May require fasting if contrast is used. Arrive 15 mins early.

Learn More →

Ultrasound - Real-time Diagnostics

Real-time, non-invasive imaging using high-frequency sound waves. Safe and highly effective for abdominal and obstetric diagnostics.

Preparation

Prep: Varies by exam. May require full bladder or fasting.

Learn More →

Digital X-Ray - Low Dose

Standard radiologic imaging enhanced by digital processing for finer cellular bone and chest assessment with minimal radiation exposure.

Preparation

Prep: No special preparation. Wear loose, comfortable clothing.

3D TOMO

Mammography - Advanced Screening

Advanced 3D tomosynthesis for early detection and comprehensive breast health screening, offering superior clarity and reduced false positives.

Preparation
