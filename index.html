Our Services

We offer a wide range of medical imaging services to help diagnose and treat various conditions.

Maternal & Fetal Care

Routine obstetric scans, Fetal Doppler (BPP), and Biophysical Profile Scoring.

Abdomen & Pelvis

Routine general assessments, trauma eFAST scans, and chest ultrasounds.

Musculoskeletal (MSK)

Targeted imaging for the knee, shoulder, wrist, elbow, and ankle joints.

Gynaecological Scans

Detailed gynaecological assessments, early pregnancy checks, and Transvaginal Scans (TVS).
