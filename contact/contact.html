Patient Data Protocols (FAQ)

How should I prepare for an MRI scan?

Preparation varies by scan type, but generally involves removing all metallic objects, wearing comfortable clothing without zippers or snaps, and potentially fasting if contrast dye is to be administered. A detailed prep sheet will be transmitted to you upon appointment confirmation.
